SOCIAL AI MANAGER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Overview

Social AI Manager is a private, single-user dashboard. It is designed to manage the owner's connected social media accounts and is not intended to collect information from the public.

Information used

The tool may process account identifiers, access tokens, scheduled post details, captions, media URLs, platform responses, and trend data needed to provide its features. If an AI provider is enabled, text submitted for caption generation may be sent to that provider to produce a response.

How information is used

  • To authenticate the dashboard owner.
  • To generate, schedule, and publish requested content.
  • To retrieve requested trend and account information from connected platforms.
  • To diagnose failures and maintain the application.

Sharing

Information is sent only to the social platforms and service providers required for a requested feature, such as an AI caption provider or an official platform API. Personal information is not sold or rented.

Storage and security

Credentials should be stored in the application's private environment configuration and must not be shared publicly. The owner should use HTTPS when exposing the dashboard to the internet, restrict access with a strong passcode, and revoke tokens that are no longer needed. Local development data may be stored in the application's data files.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained only while needed for scheduling, history, and operation of this private tool. The owner can delete local application data and revoke platform permissions through the relevant platform settings.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the private app owner through the account used to manage this application.
